ACME TECHNOLOGIES
DOCUMENT: LEAVE POLICY
POLICY ID: HR-001

1. Annual Leave
Full-time employees receive 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year.

2. Sick Leave
Full-time employees receive 10 days of paid sick leave per calendar year. Employees may be asked to provide medical documentation for absences exceeding three consecutive working days.

3. Applying for Leave
Employees must submit annual leave requests through the HR portal at least five working days before the requested leave begins. Manager approval is required before leave is confirmed.

4. Public Holidays
Employees receive paid leave on company-recognized public holidays. The official holiday calendar is published by HR at the beginning of each year.
