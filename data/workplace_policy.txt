ACME TECHNOLOGIES
DOCUMENT: WORKPLACE POLICY
POLICY ID: HR-003

1. Working Hours
The standard working schedule is Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, with a one-hour lunch break.

2. Remote Work
Employees may work remotely up to two days per week with prior manager approval. Certain roles may have additional on-site requirements.

3. Equipment
Employees must use company-approved devices to access internal company systems. Lost or stolen company devices must be reported to IT immediately.

4. Probation
New employees normally have a three-month probation period. The employee's offer letter specifies any role-specific probation conditions.
