DOCUMENT: TRAVEL AND EXPENSE POLICY
POLICY ID: HR-002

1. Eligible Expenses
Employees may claim approved business travel expenses, including transportation, accommodation, and meals during authorized business trips.

2. Submission Process
Employees must submit expense claims through the company expense portal within 15 calendar days of completing a business trip.

3. Required Evidence
Itemized receipts are required for individual expenses above ₹1,000. Claims must include the business purpose, travel dates, and relevant receipts.

4. Approval
The employee's manager must approve the claim before reimbursement is processed.

5. Processing Time
Approved expense claims are normally processed within 10 business days. Processing may take longer if additional documentation is required.
