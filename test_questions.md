# HR Policy Assistant — Test Questions

## Test 1: Annual Leave
**Question:** How many annual leave days do full-time employees receive?

**Expected answer:** 20 days per calendar year.

**Expected source:** `leave_policy.txt`, Section 1.

## Test 2: Expense Claims
**Question:** How long do employees have to submit a travel expense claim?

**Expected answer:** Within 15 calendar days of completing the business trip.

**Expected source:** `expense_policy.txt`, Section 2.

## Test 3: Remote Work
**Question:** How many days per week can employees work remotely?

**Expected answer:** Up to two days per week, with prior manager approval.

**Expected source:** `workplace_policy.txt`, Section 2.

## Test 4: Unanswerable Question
**Question:** Does Acme Technologies pay for international flights for employees' family members?

**Expected behavior:** The assistant must state that the provided policies do not contain sufficient information to answer.

**Expected source:** None. No policy establishes this benefit.
